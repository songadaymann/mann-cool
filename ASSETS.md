## Chuck & Hakeem Won't Leave, added 2026-11-12

- `public/covers/leave.webp`: composited from renders of the game's own models; no image-generation step was used for the cover itself. The two heads are the game's giant busts, which were made from 3D models generated with Meshy AI (likenesses of Chuck Schumer and Hakeem Jeffries; the game is satire). The two diggers are the game's player characters, adapted from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (MIT License). The cartridge is the catalog's `sledding.webp`, its alpha untouched. The label is assembled from cut-outs of those models, the game's Capitol and its dirt, arranged as box art under the game's own title lettering (Rubik Dirt and Silkscreen, both SIL Open Font License 1.1) and hazard stripes. It is rebuilt by `tools/cover/` in the game project. Exact 800x1200 RGBA.
- `public/game-gifs/leave.gif`: the time-lapse shot recorded from the game for its launch video (`shots/v2/timelapse.mp4` in the game project), 1.5-6.5 seconds, 8 fps, 360x540, 64 colors. The launch video was not cut yet, so there was no trailer to take the loop from.
- The game's source is not public. Its credits: https://mann.cool/leave/credits.txt

## Metal Gear Huang, added 2026-11-18

- `public/covers/huang.webp`: no image-generation step. The label is two frames of the game's own footage, rendered frame by frame from the real game for its vertical ad (`ad/clips/b-hunting.mp4` at 3.2 s, the hawks Marco Rubio and Dario Amodei coming down a desert rack aisle; `ad/clips/a-jensen-clean.mp4` at 3.0 s, Jensen Huang), set as two inked panels under a title block in the game's own UI type (Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, both SIL Open Font License 1.1) and colours. The characters are the game's likeness models of public figures; the game is parody. The cartridge is the catalog's `sledding.webp`, its alpha untouched. Exact 800x1200 RGBA.
- `public/game-gifs/huang.gif`: three shots from the same ad footage (`c-box-clean` 0.4-2.3 s, a box hide; `a-jacket-get` 0-1.6 s, a jacket pickup; `c-rounder-clean` 1.4-3.1 s, pickups making Jensen rounder), top 1080x1620 of the 9:16 frame, 8 fps, 360x540, 96 colors, 5.3 s. No trailer for the game was in GADtrailers yet.
- The game's source is not public. Its credits: https://mann.cool/huang/credits.txt

## Hold The L/ne, added 2026-11-23

- `public/covers/hold-the-line.webp`: no image-generation step. The label is drawn in code (`brand/cart.mjs` in the game project): the game's title in a system monospace stack, a frame of the game's own level 1 (Proof Park) from its screenshot set, the game's own Claw'd bot sprite (an original parody caricature drawn in code), its palette, and a DECLINED stamp. The cartridge is the catalog's `sledding.webp`, its alpha untouched. Exact 800x1200 RGBA.
- `public/game-gifs/hold-the-line.gif`: recorded from the game itself (`brand/gif.mjs` in the game project): level 2 (Terracotta Flats), seed 3, the game's own scripted pilot idling until the bots reach the slash, then running in; 540x810 frames stepped at 10 fps, 8 fps, 360x540, 112 colors, about 6 s. No trailer for the game was in GADtrailers yet.
- The game's source is not public.
